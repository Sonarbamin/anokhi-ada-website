Our Story

A Boutique Built on Sona's Eye

Sona Amin opened Anokhi Ada in 2006 with one goal: give Atlanta a place to find Indian attire that actually fits how someone wants to look, not just what's in stock. She still hand-picks every fabric herself and works directly with designers across India to bring in pieces you won't find anywhere else in the city.

No two clients leave with the same look. The boutique carries everything from richly worked saris to detailed embroidered suits to full bridal ensembles, and Sona works one-on-one with each customer to match the piece to the person. Bridal and trousseau shopping is where the boutique really specializes — traditional craft, styled for how people actually dress today.

Common Questions

Everything most people ask before their first order or appointment.

Do I need an appointment to buy something?

No — every piece in the Lookbook and Sale sections can be added straight to your bag and purchased online, no appointment required. Appointments are for private, one-on-one styling — bridal looks, custom pieces, or help putting a full outfit together.

How does booking an appointment work?

Fill out the form below or click "Request an Appointment" — we typically reply within 24 hours to find a time that works. Anokhi Ada is an appointment-only studio in Atlanta, so the exact address is shared once your visit is booked.

What's your return policy?

Every piece we carry is one-of-a-kind — once it's gone, it's gone. Because of that, all sales are final and pieces ship as-is, with no alterations included.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 7–10 business days of purchase.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payment is processed by Square, a widely used payment platform — your card details are handled directly by Square and never touch our servers.

Can I ask about sizing or fit before I buy?

Absolutely — click "Ask a Question" on any piece and we'll get back to you with measurements and fit details before you commit to buying.
