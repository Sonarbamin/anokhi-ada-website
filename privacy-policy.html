Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Anokhi Ada ("we," "us," "our") collects from visitors and customers of this website, and how that information is used.

Information We Collect

When you make a purchase or book an appointment, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your shipping address
  • Payment information (processed directly by Square — see below)
  • Any details you include in an appointment request or inquiry

How Payments Are Processed

All payments on this site are processed by Square, Inc. We do not receive, see, or store your full card number at any time — Square handles that directly. Please refer to Square's own Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill and ship your order
  • To contact you about your order or appointment
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To send occasional updates, if you've opted in, about new arrivals or promotions

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Information Sharing

We share information only as needed to complete a transaction — for example, with Square to process payment, or with a shipping carrier to deliver your order. We do not share your information for advertising or marketing purposes without your consent.

Data Retention

We retain order and customer information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, comply with tax and business record-keeping obligations, and resolve any disputes.

Your Rights

You may request to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law (such as the California Consumer Privacy Act).

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies to remember your shopping bag while you browse. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
